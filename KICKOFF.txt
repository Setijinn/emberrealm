HARD RULES — these are not preferences
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Bump CACHE in sw.js on EVERY commit, and serve on a FRESH PORT to verify
  (py -m http.server 9NNN). The trap is the browser's HTTP cache, not the SW.
- Commit AND push each change, fetching first. A collaborator shares this repo.
- Verify by DRIVING THE GAME, not by assuming. Report measurements. Say plainly
  when something fails or when you did not test it.
- LEAVE THE BLACKSMITH ALONE. Reserved for a future item-fusion system. Bram's
  stock is empty by design; the stall stays. Open design work: invent what fusion
  consumes and where it drops.
- Do NOT build player-to-player auction listings. The peer-to-peer architecture
  cannot host them honestly — no escrow, no persistence, client-editable glory.
- Do NOT attempt to collect real money for diamonds client-side.
- GLORY MUST NEVER BUY POWER, and nothing may mint spendable glory mid-run. It is
  account-level and paid only on permanent death.
- LOOT FROM A KILL IS ALWAYS A SACK. One sack per kill, band decided by the best
  thing inside it. The Emberwrought chest is the ONE exception and it is not a
  drop — it is a placed event object, and it looks like nothing else so it can
  never be mistaken for something a monster left.

COMBAT / CONTENT
- The Monk has a real weapon: 'fists' retired in place (legacy:1), 'gauntlet' added
  and droppable, with a save migration for stranded relic weapons.
- CREATURE ROSTER 18 -> 54 -> 252. Fourteen species per band in the open, and
  every DUNGEON has its own twelve on top of that. A pool is a list; which one a
  spawn point gets is hashed from the point. sp.spec names a species outright
  (that is how the dev tool spawns dungeon-only creatures anywhere).
- A DUNGEON'S ROSTER IS KEYED TO THE BOSS'S RING (DUNSPEC), not to the terrain
  band its index lands on. It used to CONCATENATE the overworld list, so the
  Windward Roost — a lighthouse crag — spawned Wolfwood wolves and three dungeons
  shared the Ashfall's list. Invariant: ZERO species appear in two dungeons, and
  zero dungeon species walk the surface. Assert it after touching MOBSPEC/DUNSPEC.
- The nightmare roster is named for what it is (Choir of Open Mouths, Stag of
  Nails, Rope of Necks) and dungeon creatures RENDER differently: body darkened,
  violet-black bleed beneath, red eyes, a per-creature shudder instead of the
  calm sine bob. Not a dream. A nightmare.
- ELITES: ~1 roaming spawn in 13 (ELITE_P=0.075), rolled from the spawn POINT so
  a spot keeps its elite across respawns. Not a new creature — the band's own
  species grown: 2.9x hp, 1.45x dmg/touch, its own title, 3x XP, one tier band
  richer on loot (zoneTierRowUp) and the only way a chaser drops an egg. Reads as
  a gold ground ring, chevrons round a gold name, and a crown. Wire bit 256.
- TWELVE ARCHETYPES carry all 252 species (crab, swarm, bird, husk, golem, plant,
  wisp, boar, slinger, shaman, acolyte, beast). e.arch is stamped at spawn BEFORE
  the elite prefix renames the species, or every elite misses MOB_ARCH by name.
  Band tint on top = 108 looks. MOBTINT entries match the ground named beside
  them now; they had drifted (index 0 is a BEACH and was tinting things green).
- EVERY CREATURE IN THE GAME IS ANIMATED: 12 archetypes idle+attack (9 frames),
  12 bosses, 68 classes/ascensions, the flock, 3 allies, all 32 pets. Pet sets
  load LAZILY per species — only the one beside you is ever probed.
- THE AGGRESSION RAMP. Shooters fire 1 shot below Lv12, 2 below Lv30, 3 beyond;
  cadence 1.61s -> 0.66s; flat damage floors dropped (shooter 8->5, touch 12->7)
  so the cut lands on the early game. Net pressure vs before: 20% at Lv1, 56% at
  Lv12, 103% at Lv30, 138% at Lv50. The top end got HARDER on purpose.
- ALL 25 BOSS FIGHTS have their own mechanic (17i_bossfights.js), built on a
  primitive library (17h_mechlib.js). 12 identities x 2 forms + arena champion.
  Both forms of a boss share a family; the dungeon form twists one element and
  adds one of its own.
- Fight difficulty scales with level through ONE dial: bossPace(e). Telegraphs
  1.00->0.55, cycles 1.00->0.62, counts 1.00->1.90, damage 1.00->1.30, and a
  dungeon form is harder again. No primitive hard-codes a timing — if you add
  one, read bossPace.
- Boss framework: fights keyed ow<ring> / dn<ring> / arena; per-fight phase
  counts (1-4 breaks, boss bar reads the same list); anchored phases where the
  boss plants at its arena centre; room.bossCh persisted so dungeon bosses
  finally have arena bounds.
- AN ANCHORED PHASE IS A TIMED WINDOW, NEVER A STATE. 9s untouchable and firing,
  then IT IS EXPOSED and 7.5s open (scaled by bossPace), then it re-plants. It is
  not immune until it ARRIVES. A fight that gates itself — cut the knots, break
  the conduits — sets e.anchorNoInv=1 and uses wardInv instead, so the two rules
  never stack. Shipped as an unbounded per-phase state first, which made twelve of
  the fifteen anchored fights literally unkillable.
- RULE FOR ANY NEW IMMUNITY: there must be an exit the player can reach. dn5
  pinned mechInv for phases 0-2 whose only exit was HP it forbade you from taking,
  so the Bog Horror could never be fought at all. It surfaces in punish windows now.
- SWEEP TEST, run it after touching any fight: spawn all 25, drive each to 0 with
  fixed DPS, assert none reads NEVER and the longest immune streak is <= 9.0s.
- A FIGHT THAT IS NOT RUNNING MAY NOT HOLD YOU IMMUNE. Every tick opens with
  `if(!eng) return;`, so a fight interrupted mid-vanish returns before it can clear
  mechInv. bossMechTick clears it on disengage; bossReset clears mechInv, bloom and
  hidden. Any new transient immunity must be cleared in BOTH places.
- THE ANCHOR'S IMMUNE WINDOW SCALES WITH bossPace TOO. Only the cooldown used to,
  so the untouchable window stayed 9s while the hittable one shrank with level:
  80% untouchable at Lv55 vs 55% at Lv4. A harder fight is FASTER, not longer.
- PHASES MUST ESCALATE, AND ESCALATING A TIMER IS NOT ESCALATING A THREAT. dn8 sped
  its closing ring up each phase, and a faster ring spends LESS time small, so every
  later phase was safer (32%/s -> 9%/s). Escalate reach, cadence AND damage.
- MEASURE A FIGHT AGAINST A MOVING PLAYER AT THE HERO'S REAL SPEED (player.spd,
  ~240px/s). A statue over-reports any fight that targets your position by 5x; a
  400px/s strafe under-reports every telegraph as zero. Both happened in one sweep.
- Intercept damagePlayer to measure. Healing after the fact lets a lethal frame end
  the test, and death exits the dungeon, which reads as "the phase does nothing".
- Boss animation: 15 shared beats (17f_bossanim.js) plus all twelve identities
  with their OWN idle, tell, speech, death and signature moves (17g_bossprofiles).
  Procedural squash/stretch/lean, scaled about the FEET. bossAnimFrames is the
  seam if hand-drawn frames ever ship.

- A DYING BOSS TAKES ITS SUMMONS WITH IT. Summons have no spawn point, so nothing
  culled them, and they carried no `owner`, so bossReset's purge could not match
  them either — a cleared dungeon kept a handful of adds standing in it. Both
  summon paths tag their adds now. The 60s streaming respawner is separately gated
  on !curRoom.dungeon: dungeons do not repopulate, verified by object identity
  over 180s of game time per dungeon, not by counting.

UI / WORLD
- Portal and loot prompts are SEPARATE controls: E uses the world, F takes loot.
  Taps test TAKE first.
- iOS zoom is locked out: * is touch-action:manipulation, canvas is none, plus JS
  backstops for WebKit pinch gestures and a canvas double-tap guard. The guard is

  which on the HUD would eat the second tap of a potion-mash.
- Boss dialogue is a FIXED subtitle bar above the orbs, with the speaker named.

  objective banner owns that strip.

  second was read).
- SPRITE SETS BUILD PER CLASS, NOT ALL AT ONCE. _emberBuild used to run exactly
  once, when the last of ~2720 probes settled, so one slow request held back all 68
  sets and every character drew the procedural fallback until the whole library was
  in. Each set assembles when its own frames land. The 51 ascension sets (1851 files,
  11MB) load LAZILY on first emberSprite request, falling through to the base class.
  Boot probes: 2720 -> 680.
- 8-bit floor detail on every surface (09c_floordetail.js): five stamp families,
  eight variants each, pre-rendered at boot. 1.32ms/frame for the whole pass.
- NO FLOOR MAY PAINT A HASHED FLAT WASH ACROSS A WHOLE TILE. Five renderers each
  had their own copy and every one drew the grid: the value was quantised so a
  seam was never a small step, and hashed so neighbours were uncorrelated and
  every edge jumped the full amplitude. They all call tileShade(tx,ty,x,y,amp,seed)
  now — continuous noise sampled at the tile CORNERS, filled as four bilinear
  quadrants, so the ramp runs through the seam. Largest adjacent jump 21.8 -> 11.8.
  The same mistake in animated form was the corruption shimmer at 1.3 rad/tile.
- A DUNGEON IS NAMED after the boss's dream (GBOSS[ring].dn). genDungeon never set
  room.name, so the "where you are" label was blank for the whole dungeon.
- THE MINIMAP has its zoom buttons INSIDE the frame, bottom-right (miniBtns() owns
  the geometry for both the painter and the hit-test). Terrain is tinted per
  character on top of the zone ramp, cache at 0.62 px/tile, plus live foes,
  objective nodes, loot, portals, ground level and a north mark — all fog-gated.
  EVERY INSTANCE HAS ONE: it reads curRoom, and the palette is chosen by what kind
  of room you are in (over / town / dun / plain) because the same character means
  different things — 'W' is a wall in the Hearth and the VOID in a dungeon. Cache
  scale follows room size (0.62-8 px/tile). The window is clamped to the room AND

  long dungeon rather than shrinking it into a two-pixel band. Fog is per-instance,
  persisted for the OVERWORLD ONLY (a dungeon is a new layout every time, so old
  fog would uncover walls that are not there); the Hearth and service rooms never

  wallhack over most of the map.
- The Hearth has grass, wildflowers, and a flock: 6 hens and 4 sheep

- THE DEV WORKBENCH (12b_devplus.js): ten tabs, ~300 controls, every list reading

CO-OP (v370-v375) -- done, with two things stated plainly
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- HANDING THE ROOM OVER IS AN EDGE, AND BOTH DIRECTIONS NEED CLEANING. netSimulates()
  flipping true->false (the host walked into your room) must drop the world the
  client already built, or you fight BOTH its locals and the host's shadows of the
  same room: measured 6 remote + 6 stale locals, still there 20s later at full HP.
  netSimEdge() runs once per frame; netDropLocal() is the mirror of netDropRemote().
- netSimulates() is POSITIONAL now: a client simulates its own room unless the
  host is actually IN it. It used to be "clients never simulate", which combined
  with netApplyWorld's room filter to leave anyone paired with a distant stranger
  in a permanently empty world -- the normal case on the public server, since it
  pairs you with whoever holds the id. Measured: 8 enemies where it was 0.
- netDropRemote() purges remote shadows on every co-op reset (GO SOLO, lost
  relay, reconnect). A remote entity has none of the fields the AI needs.
- An epoch guard stops a superseded PeerJS attempt writing into a fresh coop
  object -- it could resurrect co-op after GO SOLO or null a newer peer.
- e.bd and e.awk/e.den now cross the wire. Client hazard damage was pinned to a
  `10` fallback at EVERY level, and bossMechKey read ow<ring> inside a dungeon.
- The 25 fights ride the PRIMITIVE layer at row 15 (hazards, safe ground, beams,
  marks, wards, pillars, fog); row 16 is e.bd. 24 of 25 send something -- the Ash
  Wraith's overworld form is all real projectiles, which already synced.

ONE THING NOT PROVEN: all of the above was verified by driving both sides inside
ONE tab. That tests the code paths and NOT the transport.

THE CRASH IS FOUND (v380). It was the wildflower clumps I added to the Hearth
grass: hmix() returns an UNSIGNED 32-bit hash, but I indexed the species palette
with a SIGNED shift (`hh>>9`), which converts to int32 first -- so any hash above
2^31 came out negative, `% length` gave a negative index, and `SP[-2]` was
undefined. 43 of the Hearth's 106 grass tiles (40.6%) carried a bad index. It
threw inside render() EVERY FRAME, so the world stopped drawing: that is the
"crash". Use >>> for anything that indexes an array from a hash. Note the other
signed shifts in 08_render.js are coordinate offsets where a negative is merely
cosmetic; only the array index could throw. The diagnostic error banner is what
caught it -- it reported `sp[0]` with a repeat count of 256.

QA TRAPS THAT COST TIME THIS SESSION (on top of HANDOFF.md's list)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Size sprites by their OPAQUE BOUNDING BOX (_imgBBox), never by naturalWidth.
  PixelLab files carry transparent margin; scaling by canvas size made the relic
  sack the smallest bag in the game and the entire flock render as specks.
- Top-level `const` is a LEXICAL global, NOT a window property. `window[name]`
  lookups against const-declared images silently return undefined — that is why
  every loot sack drew as plain burlap for weeks.
- .scr centres its children; a flex container centring content taller than itself
  clips BOTH ends, so the top becomes unreachable no matter what scrollTop says.
  A child with only a max-height also gets flex-shrunk to its min-content.
- When editing files with a script, build the whole text in memory and write to a
  temp before moving it into place. Opening the target for writing first will
  TRUNCATE it if anything later throws. This destroyed 11_ui.js once this session.
- touch-action does NOT inherit. Setting it on html/body says nothing about the
  canvas or the buttons on top of it.
- Loops driven by wall clock cannot be probed by stepping a frame counter — stub
  performance.now() or they read as motionless.
- CHECK YOUR OWN DIFF STAT AFTER A SCRIPTED EDIT. A replacement meant to touch one
  function ate ~90 lines — the entire boss dialogue system and bossEngaged, whose
  absence threw on the first tick of every registered fight. `git diff --stat`
  reading "38 insertions, 126 deletions" for a 40-line rewrite is the whole tell.
- PARSE-CHECK EVERY FILE as part of the run: fetch each script[src] and
  new Function(text). Manual frame-stepping does not need the rAF loop, so it
  cannot see a syntax error or a dead loop. rAF is throttled to ZERO in a
  backgrounded tab — waiting on it hangs the tool call.
- enterRoom(key, px, py) NEEDS the coordinates. Calling it with one argument puts
  the player at NaN and solid() then throws reading grid[NaN]. Only '0,0' has
  R.px/R.py; everywhere else use safeSpot().
- CHECK THE DIFF, AND PARSE-CHECK, AFTER EVERY SCRIPTED EDIT. A replacement whose
  match ended in `}
  },` but whose replacement ended in `}` silently dropped the
  close of a tick function; 17i_bossfights.js stopped parsing and every fight fell
  back to stale definitions. The parse check caught it in one run; the game looked
  merely "wrong" without it.
- PIXELLAB animate_image TAKES BASE64 AND THE TRANSPORT TRUNCATES IT, intermittently
  and NOT by size: inputs smaller than one that just succeeded still arrived
  clipped by a few bytes. What works: downscale to 22-40px on the long side, hard
  alpha edge (soft edges cost most of the bytes), 6-16 colour palette, and retry.
  animate_object (for things PixelLab generated) has no such problem — it takes an
  id. Frames come out of the object/image DOWNLOAD endpoint as a zip; the direct
  backblaze URLs are 403.
- A PANEL FITTING ITS FRAME IS NOT THE SAME QUESTION AS scrollHeight<=clientHeight.
  equip_panel.png is 448x600 drawn at background-size:100% 100%, so the frame
  STRETCHES to its box and its usable dark field is a PERCENTAGE inset (16.5% top,
  18% bottom, ~16% sides, measured off the art) while #shopInner pads a fixed
  46/40/44px. Those agree at one card size only. The panel reported "fits" while
  visibly spilling over the border art. Measure against the art's real field:
  card.top + height*0.165 .. card.bottom - height*0.180, and check EVERY child.
- AND CHECK IT AT PHONE SIZES. The vault passed at desktop and overflowed by 87px
  at 320x568, because the card scales with the viewport and fixed-px chrome does
  not. You cannot resize this browser's viewport from the tool, but you can drive
  it directly: the card owns --vH/--vW, so set them inline, force a sync reflow
  with `void card.offsetHeight` (NOT rAF, which is throttled to zero here) and
  re-measure. Eight viewports takes one tool call.
- HARNESS BUGS OUTNUMBERED REAL BUGS in this sweep. Before reporting a finding,
  check the API you called: floorFamily takes (tileChar, room) not (x,y);
  zoneTierRow is POSITIONAL so a loot test must roll inside the zone it means;
  a Lv1 test hero dies in one frame in a Lv75 zone and the death resets the room.
