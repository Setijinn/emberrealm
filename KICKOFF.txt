HARD RULES — these are not preferences
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Bump CODE_CACHE in sw.js on every commit.
- SERVE WITH `py tools/serve.py` (fixed port 10500, no-store, kills its own
  previous instance). Do NOT go back to `python -m http.server` on a fresh port
  per edit -- that ritual leaked a process every time and one session found 232
  orphaned servers holding 3.2 GB. serve.py fixes the caching instead of dodging
  it. Verify it is actually serving before navigating: `&& echo serving` after a
  failed command prints the echo and starts nothing.
- Commit AND push each change, fetching first. A collaborator shares this repo.
- Verify by DRIVING THE GAME, not by assuming. Report measurements. Say plainly
  when something fails or when you did not test it.
- RUN `py tools/selftest.py` after touching the tier ladder, the loot tables, the
  level cap, the forge or the dev workbench. 144 checks, no node and no CDP
  needed. It finds Chrome itself ($CHROME wins, then a list, then Playwright's
  versioned download dir) and passes --no-sandbox when it is running as root.
- `py` IS BROKEN ON THIS MACHINE -- call the interpreter by its full path,
  C:\Users\darkc\AppData\Local\Programs\Python\Python312\python.exe. The `python`
  on PATH is the Microsoft Store alias stub and the launcher follows each script's
  env-python shebang straight into it. See HANDOFF.md; it reads as a broken script
  rather than a broken launcher.
- NOTHING MAY SIT ABOVE Lv50. levelUp stops at LV_CAP and there is no prestige
  level, so content that computes its own level must land on or under it. Six
  ascended dungeons were at Lv53-55 and could only ever be fought underlevelled.
- SCAVENGED DREAMS IS WRITTEN "SD", NEVER "T13". Go through tierTag().
- Do NOT build player-to-player auction listings. The peer-to-peer architecture
  cannot host them honestly — no escrow, no persistence, client-editable glory.
- Do NOT attempt to collect real money for diamonds client-side.
- GLORY MUST NEVER BUY POWER, and nothing may mint spendable glory mid-run. It is
  account-level and paid only on permanent death.
- LOOT FROM A KILL IS ALWAYS A SACK. One sack per kill, band decided by the best
  thing inside it. The Emberwrought chest is the ONE exception and it is not a
  drop — it is a placed event object, and it looks like nothing else so it can
  never be mistaken for something a monster left.

TWO NEW DIALS, AND THEY ARE DELIBERATELY SEPARATE CONSTANTS.
- PROJ_SCALE = 0.90 (01_constants.js) slows every projectile -- hero shots,
  ability and ultimate bolts, perk volleys, every enemy and boss pattern -- and
  HOLDS REACH CONSTANT. Reach in this game is speed x life and nobody ever wrote
  it down as a number, so scaling velocity alone would have cut every weapon's
  range, every boss's threat radius and the auto-aim cap by 10% -- a far bigger
  balance change hiding inside a cosmetic one. So it lands where a shot is
  ADVANCED, on the step and the lifetime together: x += vx*dt*S, life -= dt*S.
  Measured: a 600px bolt still covers 603px, taking 1.117s where it took 1.000s.
  Applied at the INTEGRATION step, not at spawn -- there are ~35 places that
  create a projectile and 3 that move one (pShots, eShots, and the client's
  dead-reckoning in 14b_netsync; miss that last one and a client's bolts drift
  10% ahead of the host's).
- RANGE_SCALE = 0.85 shortens WEAPON reach and leaves speed alone. Sword 106->90,
  Dagger 157->133, Bow 768->653, Crossbow 988->840, Staff 432->367, Wand
  840->714, Gauntlets 94->80. WEAPONS ONLY, taken literally: it scales wt.life
  in WTYPE, so ability bolts, ultimates, perk volleys and every enemy/boss
  pattern keep their designed reach -- nerfing enemy reach too would have quietly
  handed the player a safety margin under cover of a nerf. All three readers of
  reach moved together (shot lifetime, fire()'s auto-aim cap, aimPoint's
  intercept window); miss one and the reticle locks onto something the bolt
  expires short of, which reads as broken aim.
  OPEN QUESTION FOR THE USER: if "weapons" was meant to include the spells or
  the enemies, both are one line from here. Nobody has confirmed which was meant.

THE GROUND STOPPED BEING FLAT GREEN.
- A TONE CORRECTION OUTLIVED THE ART IT WAS CORRECTING. _bandTone exists because
  four PixelLab sheets came back with the wrong palette, but the later terr_N
  ATLASES have none of those faults and the fill sat OUTSIDE the atlas branch --
  so the band-0 grass wash was painted over band-0 SAND every frame and the
  Landing Sands beach rendered as olive mud, indistinguishable from Sawgrass
  Flats two provinces away. Gated on the fallback path now, which is all it was
  ever written for. z0 luma 102->145, z2 98->119, z8 49->65, the rim 29->35, and
  the seven provinces on bands with no tone entry are byte-identical.
- PROVINCES BLEND. grvBandXY warps the band SAMPLE POINT so boundaries interlock
  in fingers instead of following the exact Voronoi edge, and _bandBlendMap()
  makes the transition THREE TILES WIDE (one tile cannot join atlas luma 129 to
  51 -- the tile past the blend still steps off the cliff). The warp stays in the
  RENDERER and must never move into grvBandAt, which answers for the roster, the
  level, the minimap and the danger seam. The 2px black keyline is gone; real
  ground has no black line where it changes.
- SHADES AND BLOOMS. BAND_SHADE gives every band a drier/warmer and a
  deeper/cooler alternate along one continuous axis, so the ground is somewhere
  on it everywhere rather than in occasional patches. BAND_BLOOM grows each band
  its own thing where a low-frequency field says: flowers on grass, lichen on
  scree, ember motes in the ash.
- THREE ATLASES WERE REPAINTED, because this is the one ground problem no filter
  can reach: terr_4/5/8 measured luma ~50 with a standard deviation of FIVE --
  not merely dark but nearly FLAT, and brightening a flat field gives a brighter
  flat field. terr_4 51.9->76.0, terr_5 52.1->68.0 (lifted least on purpose; its
  own comment calls it "deep shade"), terr_8 49.3->69.1. AND terr_8 WAS THE WRONG
  COLOUR: neutral grey (47,50,47) where TERR_ACCENT has called that band "ember
  crust" (112,52,26) all along. THE REMAP IS POINTWISE and that is correctness,
  not style -- these sheets tile against themselves, so any neighbourhood op
  (blur, sharpen, added structure) breaks the wrap and puts a grid back on the
  ground. Originals kept at assets/tiles/orig/.

EVERY ASCENDED BOSS DROPS ITS OWN REAGENT, AND A SEED FORGES ONLY ITS DUNGEON.

boss and a Riftseed that could become any of the twelve relic sets off a list,

  Riftseed", this paragraph is the current one.
- Integrity now asserts: every ascended boss has exactly one signature, no
  starter boss has one, every relic dungeon has a seed, every seed is made from
  its own dungeon's material, and every seed reaches exactly that dungeon's sets.

THE CRASH IS FOUND (v380). It was the wildflower clumps I added to the Hearth
grass: hmix() returns an UNSIGNED 32-bit hash, but I indexed the species palette
with a SIGNED shift (`hh>>9`), which converts to int32 first -- so any hash above
2^31 came out negative, `% length` gave a negative index, and `SP[-2]` was
undefined. 43 of the Hearth's 106 grass tiles (40.6%) carried a bad index. It
threw inside render() EVERY FRAME, so the world stopped drawing: that is the
"crash". Use >>> for anything that indexes an array from a hash. Note the other
signed shifts in 08_render.js are coordinate offsets where a negative is merely
cosmetic; only the array index could throw. The diagnostic error banner is what
caught it -- it reported `sp[0]` with a repeat count of 256.

  ascended dungeon boss    20.2% of clears pay an SD piece  → 5.0 clears for ANY

NOT MEASURED: wall-clock. Everything above is in kills and clears, which is what

THREE STATUS-EFFECT FINDINGS, measured and deliberately NOT fixed (balance calls,
one-line each — ask before changing):
  1. DoT refresh takes best-of-both. applyStatus does s.t=max(t) and s.v=max(v)
     INDEPENDENTLY, so a 500dps/1s burn refreshed by a 10dps/8s burn gives 500dps
     for 8s. Reachable: burn value is dmg*burnHit/3 and ult burns are
     valPct*c.dmg*c.AP, so values genuinely differ by source.
  2. chill/slowT desync under boss immunity. tickStatuses freezes status timers
     while a boss is immune but e.slowT and e.stunT decrement unconditionally in
     the enemy loop — measured 2s of drift after 2s of immunity. slowF() and
     player.shatter both read slowT, so the pip outlives the real effect.
  3. "A lingering tick never lands the killing blow" is framerate-dependent. The
     guard is `if(hp<1&&hp>0) hp=1`, which only catches the (0,1) band; dt is
     clamped at 0.05 and a single burn tick at maxhp 1000 is 1.1 HP, so it steps
     straight over. At 20fps it kills.

SMALL AND KNOWN: the Fusion Altar's companion chips clip their rarity labels at
the bottom of the scroll area. Functional, untidy.

QA TRAPS THAT COST TIME THIS SESSION (on top of HANDOFF.md's list)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Size sprites by their OPAQUE BOUNDING BOX (_imgBBox), never by naturalWidth.
  PixelLab files carry transparent margin; scaling by canvas size made the relic
  sack the smallest bag in the game and the entire flock render as specks.
- Top-level `const` is a LEXICAL global, NOT a window property. `window[name]`
  lookups against const-declared images silently return undefined — that is why
  every loot sack drew as plain burlap for weeks.
- .scr centres its children; a flex container centring content taller than itself
  clips BOTH ends, so the top becomes unreachable no matter what scrollTop says.
  A child with only a max-height also gets flex-shrunk to its min-content.
- When editing files with a script, build the whole text in memory and write to a
  temp before moving it into place. Opening the target for writing first will
  TRUNCATE it if anything later throws. This destroyed 11_ui.js once this session.
- touch-action does NOT inherit. Setting it on html/body says nothing about the
  canvas or the buttons on top of it.
- Loops driven by wall clock cannot be probed by stepping a frame counter — stub
  performance.now() or they read as motionless.
- CHECK YOUR OWN DIFF STAT AFTER A SCRIPTED EDIT. A replacement meant to touch one
  function ate ~90 lines — the entire boss dialogue system and bossEngaged, whose
  absence threw on the first tick of every registered fight. `git diff --stat`
  reading "38 insertions, 126 deletions" for a 40-line rewrite is the whole tell.
- PARSE-CHECK EVERY FILE as part of the run: fetch each script[src] and
  new Function(text). Manual frame-stepping does not need the rAF loop, so it
  cannot see a syntax error or a dead loop. rAF is throttled to ZERO in a
  backgrounded tab — waiting on it hangs the tool call.
- enterRoom(key, px, py) NEEDS the coordinates. Calling it with one argument puts
  the player at NaN and solid() then throws reading grid[NaN]. Only '0,0' has
  R.px/R.py; everywhere else use safeSpot().
- CHECK THE DIFF, AND PARSE-CHECK, AFTER EVERY SCRIPTED EDIT. A replacement whose
  match ended in `}
  },` but whose replacement ended in `}` silently dropped the
  close of a tick function; 17i_bossfights.js stopped parsing and every fight fell
  back to stale definitions. The parse check caught it in one run; the game looked
  merely "wrong" without it.
- PIXELLAB animate_image TAKES BASE64 AND THE TRANSPORT TRUNCATES IT, intermittently
  and NOT by size: inputs smaller than one that just succeeded still arrived
  clipped by a few bytes. What works: downscale to 22-40px on the long side, hard
  alpha edge (soft edges cost most of the bytes), 6-16 colour palette, and retry.
