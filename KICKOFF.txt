Continue work on EmberRealm (this repo: Setijinn/emberrealm) — a static-PWA pixel-art
RPG, plain numbered JS files, no build step.

FIRST: read HANDOFF.md in the repo root. It has the full project state, how to work
on this codebase, the QA traps that have already cost real time, everything that
shipped last session, the ordered next steps, and two architectural walls not to
build past. Then read the two memory files it names.

WHERE WE ARE
Last session shipped the complete loot rework and the economy migration — 12 commits,
service worker now at emberrealm-v303:
  - Fixed two live co-op bugs: clients could never loot (grant addressing never
    matched, so pickups DESTROYED the item), and both boss branches minted duplicate
    bags on every machine.
  - Rarity stopped being power; tier is the only power axis.
  - Loot tier now comes from WHERE you are (per-area tables), not from the level of
    what you killed. Public gear caps at T8; everything above is soulbound.
  - Bags hold typed slots and open into a panel with per-item TAKE / EQUIP / COMPARE.
  - Tier is stamped into every item icon, in whichever corner the art left empty.
  - 12 dungeon relics — 12% from the dungeon boss, 1.5% from its overworld form.
  - Gold wiped entirely, selling removed with it. Glory is account-level and paid
    only on permanent death, scored from what the run accomplished.
  - Permadeath moved onto the bridge instead of a level.
  - Potions refill themselves (1 per 48s, cap 5).
  - Every item has a neutral glory value with a +/-50% listing band.
  - Loot boosters became the premium tier; the Scroll of Plunder is deleted.
  - Auction house shipped as a date-seeded house rotation.

HARD RULES
- Bump CACHE in sw.js on every commit, serve on a fresh port to verify, and commit
  AND push each change after fetching first (a collaborator shares the repo).
- LEAVE THE BLACKSMITH ALONE. It is reserved for a future item-fusion system. Open
  design work: invent the items fusion consumes and where they drop.
- Do NOT build player-to-player auction listings — the peer-to-peer architecture
  cannot host them honestly (no escrow, no persistence, editable client-side glory).
- Do NOT attempt to collect real money for diamonds client-side.
- Verify by driving the game, not by assuming. Report measurements. Say plainly if
  something fails.
