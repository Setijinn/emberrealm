---------------------------------------------------------------------------
HARD RULES — these are not preferences
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Bump CACHE in sw.js on EVERY commit, and serve on a FRESH PORT to verify
  (py -m http.server 9NNN). The trap is the browser's HTTP cache, not the SW.
- Commit AND push each change, fetching first. A collaborator shares this repo.
- Verify by DRIVING THE GAME, not by assuming. Report measurements. Say plainly
  when something fails or when you did not test it.
- LEAVE THE BLACKSMITH ALONE. Reserved for a future item-fusion system. Bram's
  stock is empty by design; the stall stays. Open design work: invent what fusion
  consumes and where it drops.
- Do NOT build player-to-player auction listings. The peer-to-peer architecture
  cannot host them honestly — no escrow, no persistence, client-editable glory.
- Do NOT attempt to collect real money for diamonds client-side.
- GLORY MUST NEVER BUY POWER, and nothing may mint spendable glory mid-run. It is
  account-level and paid only on permanent death.
- LOOT FROM A KILL IS ALWAYS A SACK. One sack per kill, band decided by the best
  thing inside it. The Emberwrought chest is the ONE exception and it is not a
  drop — it is a placed event object, and it looks like nothing else so it can
  never be mistaken for something a monster left.

COMBAT / CONTENT
- The Monk has a real weapon: 'fists' retired in place (legacy:1), 'gauntlet' added
  and droppable, with a save migration for stranded relic weapons.
- Creature roster 18 -> 54 species. Every band gained a second chaser and shooter,
  plus two that exist ONLY inside dungeons. A band's pool is a list; which one a
  spawn point gets is hashed from the point. sp.spec names a species outright
  (that is how the dev tool spawns dungeon-only creatures anywhere).
- THE AGGRESSION RAMP. Shooters fire 1 shot below Lv12, 2 below Lv30, 3 beyond;
  cadence 1.61s -> 0.66s; flat damage floors dropped (shooter 8->5, touch 12->7)
  so the cut lands on the early game. Net pressure vs before: 20% at Lv1, 56% at
  Lv12, 103% at Lv30, 138% at Lv50. The top end got HARDER on purpose.
- ALL 25 BOSS FIGHTS have their own mechanic (17i_bossfights.js), built on a
  primitive library (17h_mechlib.js). 12 identities x 2 forms + arena champion.
  Both forms of a boss share a family; the dungeon form twists one element and
  adds one of its own.
- Fight difficulty scales with level through ONE dial: bossPace(e). Telegraphs
  1.00->0.55, cycles 1.00->0.62, counts 1.00->1.90, damage 1.00->1.30, and a
  dungeon form is harder again. No primitive hard-codes a timing — if you add
  one, read bossPace.
- Boss framework: fights keyed ow<ring> / dn<ring> / arena; per-fight phase
  counts (1-4 breaks, boss bar reads the same list); anchored phases where the
  boss walks to its arena centre and goes untouchable; room.bossCh persisted so
  dungeon bosses finally have arena bounds.
- Boss animation: 15 shared beats (17f_bossanim.js) plus all twelve identities
  with their OWN idle, tell, speech, death and signature moves (17g_bossprofiles).
  Procedural squash/stretch/lean, scaled about the FEET. bossAnimFrames is the
  seam if hand-drawn frames ever ship.

ITEMS / ECONOMY
- Two flasks: rpg.pots (HP) and rpg.mpots (MP), independent 48s clocks, cap 5.
  Q drinks the tonic, G the mana flask. Auto-potion gained a sibling with its own
  OPTS.autoMana key and its own steps.
- POTIONS NO LONGER DROP AT ALL. PUB_POT deleted. 'pot' stays in NKIND at index 4
  — that index is bit-packed into the co-op bag word and renumbering would make
  an older peer read every coin sack as a tonic.
- PET EGGS ARE ITEMS now, rolled in rollLoot's extras and riding in the same sack.
  16% boss / 1.2% elite. Taking one goes to the incubator, never the satchel.
  'egg' is NKIND index 7.
- The Emberwrought chest (spawnEventChest): 35% relic, avg tier T11.2, 5.3 pieces.
- Sack panel decides for you: bagVerdict() rates each piece UPGRADE / SIDEGRADE /
  WORSE / OTHER CLASS, rows sort best-first, one primary button plus a compare
  toggle, and EQUIP BEST. The sort is a VIEW — bagTakeOne splices by index, so
  sorting the array itself makes every button take the wrong piece.

UI / WORLD
- Portal and loot prompts are SEPARATE controls: E uses the world, F takes loot.
  Taps test TAKE first.
- iOS zoom is locked out: * is touch-action:manipulation, canvas is none, plus JS
  backstops for WebKit pinch gestures and a canvas double-tap guard. The guard is
  canvas-only on purpose — preventDefault on touchend kills the synthesised click,
  which on the HUD would eat the second tap of a potion-mash.
- Boss dialogue is a FIXED subtitle bar above the orbs, with the speaker named.
- Status labels moved to an ornate top-centre banner carrying zone, level, glory,
  kills and co-op state, yielding to a compact line when the boss bar or an
  objective banner owns that strip.
- msg() QUEUES now (it used to clobber; two calls in a second meant only the
  second was read).
- 8-bit floor detail on every surface (09c_floordetail.js): five stamp families,
  eight variants each, pre-rendered at boot. 1.32ms/frame for the whole pass.
- The Hearth has grass, wildflowers, and a flock: 6 hens and 4 sheep
  (03b_critters.js). They are deliberately NOT in `enemies` — one stray
  membership and every AoE would slaughter them.
- THE DEV WORKBENCH (12b_devplus.js): ten tabs, ~300 controls, every list reading
  the live system rather than a copy. Sacks, relics, items, flasks, all 24 bosses
  in both forms, every animation beat, all 54 species, dungeons, UI/FX, balance
  readouts. Spawn lists carry the creature's real sprite.

DO THIS FIRST
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
CO-OP SERIALIZATION FOR THE 25 BOSS MECHANICS. This is a known, stated gap, not a
discovery. Enemy AI is host-only (07_update.js, netIsClient guard), so on a co-op
client none of the new hazards render or apply. Nothing desyncs and nothing
crashes — a client simply does not see them — but every fight is
single-player-correct only until this lands.

The old pools/bloom path shows the shape: serialize in netBroadcast
(14b_netsync.js), deserialize in netApplyWorld, apply client-side in netHazards.
The snapshot row is a FIXED 15-slot array carrying only `phase` and 4 bit-packed
flags of boss state, so new phase-visible state needs new slots or clients see
nothing. Everything a fight puts on the field lives on e.mk, which is one object
per boss — that is deliberate, and it is the one place to look.

Do it zone by zone, not all twelve at once, and verify with two clients.

QA TRAPS THAT COST TIME THIS SESSION (on top of HANDOFF.md's list)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Size sprites by their OPAQUE BOUNDING BOX (_imgBBox), never by naturalWidth.
  PixelLab files carry transparent margin; scaling by canvas size made the relic
  sack the smallest bag in the game and the entire flock render as specks.
- Top-level `const` is a LEXICAL global, NOT a window property. `window[name]`
  lookups against const-declared images silently return undefined — that is why
  every loot sack drew as plain burlap for weeks.
- .scr centres its children; a flex container centring content taller than itself
  clips BOTH ends, so the top becomes unreachable no matter what scrollTop says.
  A child with only a max-height also gets flex-shrunk to its min-content.
- When editing files with a script, build the whole text in memory and write to a
  temp before moving it into place. Opening the target for writing first will
  TRUNCATE it if anything later throws. This destroyed 11_ui.js once this session.
- touch-action does NOT inherit. Setting it on html/body says nothing about the
  canvas or the buttons on top of it.
- Loops driven by wall clock cannot be probed by stepping a frame counter — stub
  performance.now() or they read as motionless.
