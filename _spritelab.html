ramp

      

        

      

        

      

      

      

        

      
The ramp hue-shifts as it descends — shadows cool, highlights
        warm. That one move is what makes a hand-drawn ramp read as lit rather than dimmed,
        and it is the same idea as spritegen's ramp op.
